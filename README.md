# GTA V Cheat Codes Rumoured for GTA 6 (Not Confirmed)

These are Grand Theft Auto V Story Mode (single-player) cheat codes. They are rumoured for GTA 6. Rockstar has not confirmed any Grand Theft Auto VI cheat codes.

This repo collects GTA V Story Mode cheat codes — the PC words, in-game phone numbers, and PlayStation / Xbox button combinations — that have been going around as rumoured GTA 6 codes. The code entries are compiled from IGN and GameSpot and cross-checked against each other. It is not a GTA 6 leak, not a list of confirmed GTA 6 cheats, and not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. These codes are for GTA V single-player only. They do not work in GTA Online. In GTA V, using a cheat temporarily disables achievements and trophies for that session, though saving is still allowed (Rockstar Support, April 2024). There is no money cheat in the IGN or GameSpot GTA V lists. If a circulating “GTA 6” list includes names like PAINKILLER, BUZZOFF or COMET, those names are existing GTA V codes, not a Rockstar confirmation for GTA 6.

The machine-readable list, including source notes and dropped entries, is in [`cheats.json`](cheats.json). This file includes **33** Story Mode cheats that IGN and GameSpot both confirm at least in part. Instagram and some round-ups say “35 codes”; three extras often used to reach that number could not be cross-checked and were left out (see below).

Checked 20 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane (AEST, UTC+10).

## How to enter them (GTA V Story Mode only)

- **PC:** Press `~` (tilde; `¬` on some UK keyboards) to open the cheat console, type the word in capitals, and press Enter.
- **Phone:** Open the in-game phone (Up arrow or Up on the D-pad), open Contacts, open the keypad, dial the `1-999` number and call it. You can press `*` to redial the last number (IGN).
- **PlayStation / Xbox:** Enter the button combo during gameplay, fairly quickly, using the D-pad for directions. Do not pause. IGN lists the same sequences for PS3, PS4 and PS5, and for Xbox 360, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S.

A confirmation appears above the map if a combo is accepted. Reloading a save or restarting the game clears active cheats and restores achievements for a new session ([Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4ewFY12QOE4O7V1uWDEekt), 28 April 2024).
