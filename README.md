## How to enter a cheat

1. Finish the main story. A Cheats tab appears at the end of the Progress menu.
2. Complete the cheat's own challenge to unlock it. Progress made before the ending counts (GameSpot, Polygon, PC Gamer).
3. In free roam, press **Up** on the D-pad to open your phone.
4. Hold **L1 + R1** (PS5) or **LB + RB** (Xbox).
5. Enter the D-pad input.

Outlets word step 4 slightly differently: Eurogamer says “hold both shoulder buttons.” GameSpot, GamesRadar+ and Polygon say hold L1 + R1. Dexerto and PC Gamer word it “hold L1 and press R1.” Polygon says it only tested PS5 and assumes LB + RB on Xbox.

Journalist guides also report that using a cheat disables saving and trophies or achievements for that session (Eurogamer, Polygon, GameSpot, GamesRadar+, PC Gamer).

## Source disagreements

- **Raise Wanted Level:** Dexerto lists an 8-input string: Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Up, Right, Up. GameSpot, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer agree on the 7-input version shown above.
- **Unlimited Focus:** Dexerto lists Down, Up, Up, Down, Up, Down, Up, Up, Down, Right (8th and 9th inputs swapped). Eurogamer, Polygon, GameSpot, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer agree on the version shown above.
- **Attack Force:** GameSpot lists an 8-input string missing one Left: Down, Up, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Down. Eurogamer, Polygon, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer agree on the 9-input version shown above.
- **Cycle Weather:** Dexerto lists an 8-input string ending Right, Right. GameSpot, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer agree on the 7-input version shown above. Unlock condition also differs: reveal the entire map (Eurogamer, Polygon, GamesRadar+) vs finish the story (GameSpot, Dexerto).
- **$250,000 unlock:** Finish the story (GameSpot, GamesRadar+, Dexerto) vs make $250,000 from fenced goods (Polygon). Eurogamer and PC Gamer mark the unlock TBA. The input string agrees everywhere it is published.
- **Max Ammo unlock:** Most outlets say 25 bronze medals or higher at shooting ranges; PC Gamer says 20. The input string agrees.
- **Yesteryear unlock spelling:** Discover the Ridgway; Dexerto and Polygon spell it Ridgeway.
- **Entry method:** See the wording differences under “How to enter a cheat.”
