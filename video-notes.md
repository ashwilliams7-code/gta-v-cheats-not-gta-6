## What not to put in the reel

- Do not invent or show a "GTA 6" code that is not on this GTA V list.
- Do not show money glitches, trainers, mod menus, or anything in GTA Online.
- Do not use IGN's `BANDIT` / BMX spawn, GameSpot's `JRTALENT` Director Mode code, or IGN's `1-999-367-3767` "random explosion" number — they could not be cross-checked.
- Do not put the Rapid GT button combo on screen. IGN and GameSpot disagree (GameSpot starts with an extra R2 / RT). The PC word `RAPIDGT` and phone `1-999-727-4348` agree, but this reel does not need that car.
- No YouTube links, and no channel or personal branding on the video or in the repo-facing copy.
